The build card is the one page everyone at the Khan session filled in by hand. You start with the learner, the goal and the barrier. The prompt comes third.

## The card

Print it, or copy the fields into a doc you reuse.

**Context (two minutes)**

- My lesson and age band:
- What learners need to explain or do:
- The barrier I observed:
- Source or existing resource:
- What AI may help create:
- What learners must do themselves:
- Evidence I will collect:

**Build**

- Pathway (pick one): reading access, decision activity, project support
- The prompt I used (from [the prompt set](/edubuilds/#prompts)):
- What I checked against the source:
- What I changed:
- Ready for a trial? (see below)

## The twelve-minute build

- Minutes 1 to 2: fill in the context block. It's the same block that goes into [your project space](/library/classroom-project-setup-guide/), so you only write it once.
- Minutes 3 to 6: draft from the source and the matching prompt, or from a resource you already teach.
- Minutes 7 to 10: read the draft against the source and the learner action you want. Every claim, every practice word, every instruction.
- Minutes 11 to 12: save or print.

If you don't have an approved account, or the tool fails, revise a printed draft by hand. The judgment is the same.

## The three pathways

- **Reading access.** Adapt a short passage to one named barrier and keep the learning target the same. Prompt: [adapt a reading passage](/library/adapt-a-reading-passage/).
- **Decision activity.** One choice, a consequence tied to a source fact, one invented rule labeled as invented. Prompt: [decision activity or project support](/library/decision-activity-or-project-support/).
- **Project support.** An organizer or guide that sends learners back to the source and leaves the reasoning to them. Same prompt, organizer option.

## What ready means

A resource is ready for a small classroom trial when its key claims are checked, its task matches the learning goal, its supports fit the learner need, and you can say what evidence you'll look for. Write down anything still unresolved. One lesson tests the resource. It doesn't prove a learning gain.

After the trial, use the [peer review card and one-lesson trial log](/library/peer-review-card-and-trial-log/).
