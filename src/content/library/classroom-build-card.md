The build card is the one object every participant in the Khan session filled in by hand. It works because it refuses to let you start with the prompt. You start with the learner, the goal and the barrier, and the prompt comes third.

## The card

Print it, or copy the fields into a doc you reuse.

**Context (two minutes)**

- My lesson and age band:
- What learners need to explain or do:
- The observed barrier:
- Source or existing resource:
- What AI may help create:
- What learners must do themselves:
- Evidence I will collect:

**Build**

- Pathway (one): reading access, decision activity, project support
- The prompt I used (from [the prompt set](/edubuilds/#prompts)):
- What I checked against the source:
- What I changed:
- Ready for a trial? (see the definition below)

## The twelve-minute build

- Minutes 1 to 2: fill the context block. This is the same block that goes into [your project space](/library/classroom-project-setup-guide/), so you only write it once.
- Minutes 3 to 6: draft from the source and the matching prompt, or from a resource you already teach.
- Minutes 7 to 10: inspect the draft against the source and the intended learner action. Every claim, every practice word, every instruction.
- Minutes 11 to 12: save or print.

No approved account, or the tool fails? Take the paper route: revise a prepared output by hand. It teaches the same judgment.

## The three pathways

- **Reading access.** Adapt a short passage to one named barrier, with the learning target held fixed. Prompt: [adapt a reading passage](/library/adapt-a-reading-passage/).
- **Decision activity.** One choice, a consequence tied to a source fact, one invented rule labeled as invented. Prompt: [decision activity or project support](/library/decision-activity-or-project-support/).
- **Project support.** An organizer or guide that sends learners back to the source and leaves the reasoning to them. Same prompt, organizer option.

## The definition of ready

A resource is ready for a limited classroom trial when its key claims are checked, its task matches the learning goal, its supports fit the learner need, and you can say what evidence you will look for. Write down anything still unresolved. Classroom use is the next test, not proof.

After the trial, use the [peer review card and one-lesson trial log](/library/peer-review-card-and-trial-log/).
